How to Play Connect 4 Online

Connect Four is a two-player game. The goal is to connect four of your colored pieces in a row before your opponent!

  • Player 1 uses red pieces.
  • Player 2 uses yellow pieces.
  • Take turns dropping one piece into a column.
  • Pieces fall to the lowest available space.
  • Connect four pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  • If the board fills up and nobody connects four, the game is a tie.
